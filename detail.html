Buy Sell Rent Business Management
01 · Overview

What is AutoGO?

AutoGO is a full-stack automobile marketplace designed to bring vehicle buying, selling, renting, and rental business management together in one platform.

The platform allows individual users to discover vehicles, sellers to publish listings, vehicle owners to offer rentals, and rental businesses to manage their vehicles, customers, bookings, staff, and business operations from a dedicated dashboard.

The system also introduces AI-powered vehicle inspection, real-time communication, role-based access, rental booking management, analytics, and administrative tools.

02 · The Problem

What Problems Do Users Face?

Automobile marketplaces can make it easy to discover vehicles, but the complete vehicle ownership and rental journey can still involve multiple disconnected tools and manual processes.

01

Buying & Selling

Buyers need vehicle information, images, pricing, condition, location, and direct communication before making a decision. Sellers also need a simple way to publish and manage their listings.

02

Vehicle Rentals

Rental owners need more than a vehicle listing. They need to manage availability, rental prices, booking requests, customers, deposits, and booking status.

03

Manual Rental Management

Small rental businesses may rely on spreadsheets, messages, phone calls, or separate tools to track vehicles and reservations, making it difficult to manage everything in one place.

04

Vehicle Condition

Buyers and renters may have difficulty understanding a vehicle's condition from basic listing information and photographs alone.

05

Communication

Users often move between listing platforms and external communication channels to ask questions, negotiate, or discuss bookings.

06

Business Operations

Rental businesses need tools for inventory, customers, employees, bookings, reports, and daily operations rather than only a public vehicle listing page.

03 · The Solution

How AutoGO Solves These Problems

AutoGO was designed as more than a vehicle listing website. It combines the public marketplace experience with rental operations and business management tools.

01

One Platform for Buy, Sell & Rent

Users can discover vehicles for sale or rent from the same marketplace. Sellers can publish vehicles for sale while owners can offer vehicles with rental pricing and availability.

02

Complete Rental Booking System

Rental owners can define daily, weekly, and monthly rates, manage availability, receive booking requests, confirm or reject reservations, and track the complete booking lifecycle.

03

Business Management Dashboard

Rental businesses get a dedicated workspace where they can manage vehicle inventory, rental operations, customers, staff, reports, and business settings without depending entirely on manual processes.

04

AI-Powered Vehicle Inspection

Vehicle images can be analyzed using Google Gemini Vision AI to provide information about visible damage, vehicle condition, and estimated market value in PKR.

05

Real-Time Communication

Buyers, sellers, renters, and vehicle owners can communicate directly through real-time messaging with unread notifications and read receipts.

06

Centralized Platform Management

Administrators can monitor users, listings, bookings, platform activity, and analytics through a dedicated management dashboard.

04 · Rental Business

How a Rental Business Can Use AutoGO

AutoGO is designed not only for individual vehicle owners, but also for businesses that manage multiple rental vehicles. A business can use the platform as a central system for managing its rental operations.

01

Add Inventory

Business owners can add their vehicles to the system with images, specifications, pricing, condition, and rental information.

02

Set Rental Rates

Businesses can define daily, weekly, and monthly rental pricing and configure the rental information for each vehicle.

03

Manage Availability

Vehicle availability can be tracked so businesses can avoid accepting conflicting reservations for the same vehicle.

04

Receive Bookings

Customers can request a vehicle for specific dates while the business reviews the request and manages its booking status.

05

Manage Customers

Businesses can keep track of customers associated with their rental operations and manage customer-related information.

06

Manage Staff

Business operations can include staff management so the platform can support teams rather than only individual owners.

07

Monitor Reports

Businesses can use reports and analytics to understand their rental activity and overall business performance.

08

Manage Settings

Business-specific settings provide a centralized place to configure and manage the rental operation.

06 · Core Features

What I Built

Buy & Sell Vehicles

Users can create, manage, search, filter, and explore vehicle listings with pricing, specifications, images, condition, and location.

Rental Marketplace

Vehicle owners can publish rental vehicles with daily, weekly, and monthly rates and manage rental availability.

Rental Booking Engine

Includes date-conflict detection, booking requests, status management, deposit information, email notifications, and printable booking slips.

AI Vehicle Inspection

Google Gemini Vision analyzes uploaded vehicle photos to identify visible damage, assess condition, and estimate market value.

Real-Time Chat

Pusher WebSockets provide real-time communication with unread notifications, read receipts, and polling fallback.

Admin Dashboard

Administrators can manage users, listings, bookings, moderation, analytics, and platform activity.

Business Dashboard

Rental businesses can manage inventory, rentals, customers, staff, reports, and business settings.

Responsive Experience

The platform is designed for mobile, tablet, and desktop devices with responsive layouts and user-friendly workflows.

Listing Workflow

A multi-step listing process helps users enter vehicle information and upload images through a drag-and-drop interface.

Direct Contact

Users can connect through real-time chat as well as direct WhatsApp and email communication.
