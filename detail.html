AutoMarket

AI-Powered Automobile Marketplace

Project Overview

AutoMarket is a modern automobile marketplace built for buying, selling, and renting cars and bikes. The platform connects buyers, sellers, vehicle owners, and renters through a single digital marketplace.

The platform combines vehicle listings, rental bookings, AI-powered inspection, secure payments, real-time communication, and business management into one scalable system.

Core Features

Buy & Sell Vehicles

Create and manage vehicle listings with images, pricing, specifications, condition, and location.

Vehicle Rentals

Owners can list vehicles for rent with daily, weekly, and monthly rental options.

AI Vehicle Inspection

AI-powered image analysis helps identify vehicle information and inspection details from uploaded photos.

Advanced Search

Search vehicles using filters such as make, price, condition, location, and listing type.

Secure Bookings

Renters can request bookings while owners manage availability and booking status.

Real-Time Chat

Buyers, sellers, owners, and renters can communicate directly through real-time messaging.

Admin Dashboard

Administrators can manage users, listings, bookings, revenue, and overall platform activity.

Business Dashboard

Businesses can manage inventory, rentals, customers, staff, reports, and settings.
