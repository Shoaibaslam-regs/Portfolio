Aqua — Water Shop Finder & Booking SaaS

Aqua is a responsive water delivery platform that helps customers discover nearby filtered-water shops, compare product prices, select quantities, and place orders for delivery. It provides dedicated dashboards for customers, shopkeepers, and administrators to manage orders, inventory, shop approvals, subscriptions, and platform operations.

Next.js 16 React 19 TypeScript MongoDB Mongoose Tailwind CSS

The Problem

Finding nearby filtered-water shops, comparing prices, checking product availability, and placing orders can be inconvenient. Water shop owners also need a centralized way to manage products, stock, orders, and subscriptions, while administrators need control over shop approvals and platform operations.

The Solution

Aqua brings customers, water shops, and platform administrators together in one system. Customers can discover nearby shops using location-based search, compare products, and place orders. Shopkeepers can manage their inventory and orders, while administrators oversee shop approvals, subscription payments, users, and platform settings.

Core Features

  • Location-based discovery of nearby water shops.
  • Product browsing, price comparison, and quantity selection.
  • Shopping cart and cash-on-delivery ordering.
  • Shopkeeper dashboard for products, stock, and order management.
  • Admin dashboard for shop approval, user management, and platform oversight.
  • Subscription plans with manually reviewed payments.
  • Customer accounts, order history, and order status tracking.
  • Shop ratings, reviews, and verified trust badges.
  • Sales analytics and CSV report exports.
  • Role-based access control and secure authentication.

How It Works

  1. Shopkeepers register and set up their water shops.
  2. Administrators review shops before they appear in public search.
  3. Customers discover nearby approved shops and browse available products.
  4. Customers select products, choose quantities, and place orders.
  5. The system checks delivery distance, product prices, and stock availability.
  6. Shopkeepers manage incoming orders and update their statuses.
  7. Administrators manage users, subscriptions, approvals, and platform settings.

Shop & Subscription Management

Aqua provides shopkeepers with tools to manage their products, inventory, orders, and subscription payments. Shop visibility can be controlled through administrator approval and subscription settings. Administrators can review subscription payment references, manage shop status, and monitor platform activity.

Security & Reliability

  • JWT-based authentication using secure HTTP-only cookies.
  • Role-based authorization for customers, shopkeepers, and administrators.
  • Server-side validation using Zod schemas.
  • Ownership checks to protect customer and shopkeeper data.
  • Stock reservation and safeguards against conflicting order status updates.
  • Rate limiting, CSRF protection, and administrative audit logs.

Technology Stack

Frontend: Next.js App Router, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS

Backend: Next.js Route Handlers and service-based architecture

Database: MongoDB and Mongoose

Validation: Zod

Location: Geospatial queries, Leaflet, and OpenStreetMap

Testing: Vitest and MongoDB integration tests

Deployment: Next.js-compatible hosting

Project Highlights

  • Three dedicated dashboards for customers, shopkeepers, and administrators.
  • Geospatial shop discovery with product and price filters.
  • Inventory-aware ordering and delivery-radius validation.
  • Subscription management with administrator approval.
  • Secure, maintainable architecture with reusable services and validation.
  • Automated tests for business logic and database operations.
